# Phase 3 — AI Collaboration

Phase 3 connects the Observer, Co-Author, Character, World, and Analyst brains to the existing OpenAI backend.

## Flow
1. The Observer refreshes local story memory.
2. The Character Brain separates author-controlled and AI-controlled characters.
3. The World Brain supplies world and timeline context.
4. The Co-Author requests a continuation.
5. The Analyst validates continuity, pacing, grammar, and reader experience.
6. The author reviews the result and chooses Accept, Rewrite, or Delete.

The AI is explicitly prohibited from writing dialogue, thoughts, emotions, decisions, reactions, or actions for author-controlled characters.
