# Her Phase 4 — Intelligent Workspace

Phase 4 combines the five-brain engine, automatic knowledge extraction, and AI collaboration inside a simplified writing workspace.

## Interface changes

- Navigation reduced to Books, Write, Characters, World, Ideas, Insights, and Settings.
- The writing screen centers the manuscript instead of a large collection of AI buttons.
- A live knowledge strip displays discovered characters, locations, ideas, and insights.
- Secondary AI commands are grouped under AI Tools.
- Story Insights combines Observer findings and active story threads.
- AI continuation controls are Accept, Rewrite, Undo Rewrite, and Delete.

## Design rule

The author should be able to write continuously while Her learns, analyzes, and prepares collaboration in the background.
